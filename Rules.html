Minecraft Mastery Rules

1. Standardized Testing

Players must complete official Minecraft Mastery challenges under the specified conditilons.

2. No Cheating

players must not use unauthorized modifications, cheats, exploits, or outside assistants during tests.

3. Equal Conditions.

Players taking the same test should have the same starting conditions whenever possible.

4. Scoring

Individual tests produce scores from 0 to 100. These scores are combined to create category scores.

5. Overall Score

The 15 major category scores are combined to create the player's overall Minecraft Mastery score.

6. Proof

Official tests may require a video or other evidence to verify a player's performance.

7. Fair Play

Players are expected to follow the rules and compete honestly.
